# Facts and figures

**Applies to: the national minimum wage from 1 March 2026 and the earnings threshold from 1 May
2026.** Both usually change once a year. The other rules come from the Acts and stay until the
law changes.

Every figure below comes from the linked official page, on the date shown. Lessons point here
instead of repeating numbers, so updating this file updates the course. "BCEA" is the Basic
Conditions of Employment Act, "LRA" the Labour Relations Act, "EEA" the Employment Equity Act.

## National minimum wage

Source: [National Minimum Wage Amendment, Government Gazette 54075](https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/202602/54075rg11941gon7083.pdf), binding from 1 March 2026, checked 2026-11-11.

| Who | Minimum (R) |
| --- | --- |
| Most workers, per ordinary hour | 30.23 |
| Farm workers, per hour | 30.23 |
| Domestic workers, per hour | 30.23 |
| Expanded Public Works Programme workers, per hour | 16.62 |
| Learnerships | Weekly allowances in Schedule 2 of the same gazette |

Some sectors (contract cleaning, wholesale and retail, and others) have higher sectoral minimums
in the same gazette.

## Earnings threshold

Source: [BCEA Determination: Earnings Threshold, Government Gazette 54544](https://www.labour.gov.za/DocumentCenter/Regulations%20and%20Notices/Notices/Basic%20Conditions%20of%20Employment/Basic%20Conditions%20of%20Employment%20Act_Determination%20Earnings%20Threshold2026.pdf), from 1 May 2026, checked 2026-11-11.

- **R269 600.90 a year** in regular pay before deductions (overtime, subsistence and transport
  allowances do not count).
- If you earn more than this, the BCEA rules on hours, overtime, meal breaks, rest, Sunday pay,
  night work and some public holiday rules (sections 9 to 18) don't automatically protect you.
  Your contract decides them.
- If you earn this or less, you can take a claim for unpaid money to the CCMA (BCEA section 73A,
  [Act 7 of 2018](https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/201811/42059gon1302act7of2018.pdf)).

## Working hours and overtime

Source: [BCEA sections 9, 10, 16 and 18](https://www.gov.za/documents/basic-conditions-employment-act), checked 2026-11-11. Only for people earning up to the earnings threshold.

| Rule | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Ordinary hours | 45 a week; 9 a day (5 days a week or fewer) or 8 a day (more than 5 days) |
| Overtime | Only by agreement; at most 3 hours a day and 10 hours a week |
| Overtime pay | At least 1.5 × normal wage (or paid time off, by agreement) |
| Sunday work | 2 × wage; 1.5 × if you normally work Sundays |
| Public holiday you'd normally work | Only by agreement; at least 2 × the normal day's pay if you work it |

## Leave

Sources: [BCEA sections 20, 22, 25 and 27](https://www.gov.za/documents/basic-conditions-employment-act); [Labour Laws Amendment Act 10 of 2018](https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/201811/42062gon1305act10of2018.pdf); [Van Wyk v Minister of Employment and Labour [2025] ZACC 20](https://www.saflii.org/za/cases/ZACC/2025/20.pdf), all checked 2026-11-11.
Leave rules apply if you work at least 24 hours a month for the employer.

| Leave | Minimum |
| --- | --- |
| Annual | 21 consecutive days on full pay each year (15 working days for a 5-day week), or by agreement 1 day for every 17 days worked |
| Sick | Paid days equal to 6 weeks of your normal working days, per 36-month cycle; 1 day per 26 days worked in your first 6 months |
| Sick note | Employer may ask for a medical certificate if you're off more than 2 days in a row, or more than twice in 8 weeks |
| Family responsibility | 3 paid days a year when your child is sick or a close family member dies; only after 4 months' work, if you work at least 4 days a week |
| Parental (since 3 October 2025, interim court order) | A single parent: at least 4 consecutive months. Two employed parents: 4 months and 10 days to share between them |

Parental leave is unpaid by the employer unless your contract says otherwise; UIF can pay a
benefit (see UIF benefits). The court order applies until Parliament changes the BCEA.

## Notice and severance

Source: [BCEA sections 37 and 41, as amended by Act 11 of 2002](https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/201409/a11-02.pdf), checked 2026-11-11.

| Time employed | Minimum notice |
| --- | --- |
| 6 months or less | 1 week |
| More than 6 months, up to 1 year | 2 weeks |
| 1 year or more (or a farm or domestic worker employed more than 6 months) | 4 weeks |

- Notice must be in writing (unless the worker can't read).
- Retrenchment (dismissal for the employer's operational needs): severance pay of at least
  **1 week's pay for each completed year** of service.

## UIF contributions

Sources: [SARS: Unemployment Insurance Fund](https://www.sars.gov.za/types-of-tax/unemployment-insurance-fund/); [UIF basic guide](https://www.labour.gov.za/DocumentCenter/Publications/Unemployment%20Insurance%20Fund/Basic%20Guide%20to%20the%20Unemployment%20Insurance%20Fund%20(UIF).pdf), checked 2026-11-11.

| Item | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Your contribution | 1% of your pay |
| Your employer's contribution | 1% of your pay |
| Pay counted, at most (earnings ceiling, since 1 June 2021) | R17 712 a month |
| Biggest contribution each | R177.12 a month |

Workers who work fewer than 24 hours a month for an employer are not covered.

## UIF benefits

Sources: [Fact sheet on calculating UIF benefits](https://www.labour.gov.za/DocumentCenter/Publications/Unemployment%20Insurance%20Fund/Fact%20Sheet%20on%20the%20Calculation%20of%20normal%20UIF%20benefit.pdf); [UIF basic guide](https://www.labour.gov.za/DocumentCenter/Publications/Unemployment%20Insurance%20Fund/Basic%20Guide%20to%20the%20Unemployment%20Insurance%20Fund%20(UIF).pdf); [uFiling](https://ufiling.labour.gov.za/), checked 2026-11-11.

| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Credit days | 1 day of benefit for every 4 days you worked and contributed, up to 365 days |
| Unemployment benefit | A share of your average daily pay over the last 6 months: about 38% at the earnings ceiling, rising towards 60% for the lowest earners |
| Maternity, illness, parental, adoption | Up to 66% of your pay (counted up to the ceiling), less any pay your employer still gives you |
| Deadline: unemployment or illness | Within 6 months of the job ending (apply as soon as you can) |
| Deadline: maternity | Within 6 months of the birth |
| Turned down? | You can appeal on form UI 12 |
| Toll-free line | 0800 843 843 |

## Dispute time limits

Sources: [CCMA info sheet: Conciliation](https://www.ccma.org.za/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/Conciliation-Information-Sheet-2020-01.pdf); [CCMA info sheet: Late referrals and condonation](https://www.ccma.org.za/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/Late-referrals-Condonation-Applications-Info-Sheet-2018-01.pdf); [CCMA info sheet: Harassment](https://www.labour.gov.za/DocumentCenter/Publications/Operations/i-DEAL%20Magazine/2025%20EE%20Roadshow%20documents/CCMA-I832-2025-01%20Preventing%20and%20Eliminating%20Harassment%20in%20the%20workplace%20Info%20Sheet%2014%20May%202025%20final.pdf), checked 2026-11-11.

| Dispute | Refer to the CCMA (or your bargaining council) within |
| --- | --- |
| Unfair dismissal | 30 days of the dismissal, or of the final decision on an internal appeal |
| Unfair labour practice | 90 days of the act, or of when you found out about it |
| Unfair discrimination or harassment | 6 months of the act |
| Asking for arbitration after failed conciliation | 90 days after the certificate that the dispute is unresolved |

- Count every day, including weekends and public holidays. Leave out the first day, count the
  last. Dismissed on 6 August: the last day to refer is 5 September.
- The CCMA must try to conciliate within 30 days of getting your referral.
- Late? You must apply for condonation (permission), explaining why. It is not automatic.
- Conciliation and arbitration at the CCMA are free.
